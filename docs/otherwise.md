Definitions and Functions
Determinism gets confused with fatalism, with resignation, and with an excuse, let's separate them all.
I hope this hands you back your effort without handing you back the guilt, and puts you to work on something nobody is going to clap for.


Chains
Determinism is not the claim that your parents decided this. It's the claim that nothing started with you.

Two claims get swapped constantly, so pull them apart;
    Fatalism says the outcome arrives no matter what you do. Your effort is bypassed.
    Determinism says the outcome arrives through what you do. Your effort is the mechanism.
Fatalism deletes you from the chain. Determinism puts you in it and denies that you started it.

You do generate choices. You generate them the way a river generates a bend;
    out of the ground it was given,
    at the speed it was pushed,
    into the shape those two allow.
    None of that is fake. The bend is really there, and the bend is what does the carving.

The Arithmetic
The chain behind the decision you made this morning;
    13,800,000,000 years of it, since the first particles.
    86,000,000,000 neurons running it, not one of which you specified.
    3,100,000,000 base pairs of instructions you did not write.
    6,570 days of childhood you did not schedule.
Now name one input to that decision that you authored.
    Take your time.

The Bottom
It does not matter which physics wins. None of them hand you the pen.

Determinism assumes the chain has no author-shaped gaps in it. Test the assumption, three ways;
    It is possible the chain is fully determined, and the future is already implied by the past. Though nothing can compute that future faster than running it, so nobody gets the answer early, including you.
    It is possible there is real randomness at the bottom. Though a dice roll you did not throw is not an author either. Noise is not authorship.
    It is possible some of the processing is non local, and the brain is an interface rather than the whole machine. Though that relocates the machinery without relocating its causes.

Every branch lands in the same place;
    the pen stays out of your hand,
    your link stays in the chain,
    and what you do today still runs through you to get to tomorrow.

This is the exit most people take, so it's worth closing early. "Quantum, therefore free will" is a sentence that sounds like an argument. It is a change of subject.

Uncaused and random are not the same word, and neither one of them means you.

The Bill
Guilt and pride are the same billing error, with opposite signs.

You are a Link(a cause that was itself caused).
You are not an Origin(a cause that was not caused).
Every ache in this topic comes from one mislabel, so watch it run twice;
    Mislabeling a link as the origin, in a machine, results in kicking the printer.
    Mislabeling a link as the origin, in a person, results in guilt.

One error, four costumes;
    Guilt. You bill yourself for a road that was never built.
    Pride. You bill yourself for a road you also did not build.
    Blame. You bill your neighbor, who was exactly as caused as you were.
    Envy. You compare your output to theirs and skip the line where the Provisions are itemized.

Plug
Take your sharpest regret. Say it out loud: "I should have done otherwise."
    Now say what would have had to be different for that to happen. Keep going until you hit something you were issued.
    You will get there fast. That speed is the tell.

Provisions
Nobody has ever earned their starting inventory.

Provisions(what you were issued to work with) is everything that arrived before your first decision;
    the genome, the century, the language, the parents, the neighborhood,
    the health, the teachers, the accidents, the ten thousand hours somebody else paid for...
    and character(the appetite you have for using any of it).
    That last item is the one people leave off the list, and it is the one that spends the rest.

Two people get different inventories and produce different outputs, and we call the gap character, as if character arrived from outside the chain.
    Some of it is character. Character is an issued item that you can add to.
    Not issued and finished. Issued and extendable. Those are two different claims and only the second one does any work.

He has created us for a life of good deeds, which he has already prepared for us to do. — Ephesians 2:10
    Read it as engineering, not as comfort. The works were provisioned before the worker.
    God's divine power has given us everything we need. — 2 Peter 1:3
    Issued, not earned. Which is why the correct response to a good inventory is not pride. It's deployment.

Ceiling
"I could have done more today" is a claim about a number you have never measured.

Today's Ceiling(the most your provisions allowed today) is a real quantity and an unknown one.
    You cannot read it in advance. Nobody can.
    You can only read it afterward, and only by having hit it.
    So "I could have done more" is not a memory. It's a guess about a road that closed at midnight.

Capacity is not a switch. It's a dial, and the dial moves;
    (1) Under the ceiling, and you know it. You stopped early and felt it. That's a signal, not a moral event.
    (2) Under the ceiling, and you don't know it. The common case. This is what tomorrow is for.
    (3) At the ceiling. Rare, unmistakable, and always cheaper than it looked from outside.
    (4) Past the ceiling. Not heroism. Borrowing against tomorrow's reading at a terrible rate.

Yesterday's ceiling and today's are different numbers. That is the entire hope in this document.
    Tomorrow you find out if you can do more.

Probe
Effort is not a lever. It's an instrument.

You have been told effort causes the result. Mostly it measures the ceiling.
    A Probe(an attempt whose job is to measure, not to prove) is one day, run honestly, all the way to the end.
    It does not have to succeed to return a reading.
    It does have to be honest, or the reading is garbage and you will plan tomorrow on garbage.

Examine me, O God, and know my mind; test me, and discover my thoughts. — Psalm 139:23
    That is a request to be measured. Not praised, not excused. Measured.
    Study it day and night. — Joshua 1:8
    Day and night is a sample rate.

Fun fact: nobody has ever punished a thermometer for a heatwave, and nobody has ever fixed one by shouting at it. We do check it every morning.

The conclusion most people draw from all this is "so don't try," and the physics is right while the arrow is backwards.
    If you are not the origin, effort is not a moral exam you can fail.
    It is the only instrument you have for finding out what you are.
    Run the Probe !!!

Plug
Tonight, write one line: what did today's reading say?
    Not "was I good." What did it say.

The Account
Responsibility survives. Desert does not.

Here is the standard definition;
    "An agent is morally responsible for an action when they deserve praise or blame for it." 🤷
    Deserve is carrying that whole sentence, and it is smuggling a road in with it. 🤢
    Desert(deserving to suffer for it) requires that you could have done otherwise. Delete the road and the word has nothing left to point at.

So rebuild it in tiers, and count what survives;
    Causal. Your link was in the chain and the chain ran through you. Always true. Measurable.
    Account. A record exists of what your link did with what it was issued. True, and readable.
    Repair. You are the cheapest available node to fix it. Often true, and the only tier that does any work.
    Desert. You should suffer for it, because you could have chosen otherwise. ❌ No referent.

Three of the four stand. The one we delete is the one that only ever produced pain.

It is to him that we must all give an account of ourselves... we have a High Priest who was tempted in every way that we are. — Hebrews 4:13-15
    The account is real. The auditor ran your provisions.
    That isn't sentiment, it's a specification: the one reading your ledger has been inside the constraints written on it.
    Approach... where there is grace.

You, Lord, give perfect peace to those who keep their purpose firm. — Isaiah 26:3
    Peace is what's left when you stop being billed as the Origin. It is not the same thing as being let off.

Implications and Applications
Quiet Load
The work that matters least to the room is usually holding the most weight.

Applause is a signal and it measures the wrong thing.
    Not the wrong thing. The wrong axis.
    It measures how legible(easy to admire from a distance) the work is, not how much of the future is resting on it.
    Legible and load-bearing are two different axes, and almost nobody plots them against each other.

Quiet Load(essential work nobody claps for) is the region where those two come apart;
    the sewer, the thread standard, the shipping container, the error correcting code,
    the null check, the backup nobody needed until the morning they did,
    the boring format everyone agreed on so that everything after it could exist...
    Every one of them dull. Every one of them holding up a floor you are standing on.

If your project needs an audience to stay alive, you have two projects and one of them is a costume.
    Pick the work by the load. Let the applause find it or not.
    Waiting for validation is the same billing error again, outsourced: a crowd hired to confirm you're an Origin.

Plug
Name the piece of your work that would break the most if you deleted it tomorrow.
    Now name the piece that gets the most compliments.
    If they're the same item, say so out loud. It's rare and worth knowing.

The Handoff
I am not afraid of being gone. I am afraid of this being unfinished.

I think about dying and the fear doesn't point where people expect it to.
    It isn't the dark, and it isn't pain, and I don't need to be present for the good part.
    It's that a world like this one would keep going without the work I was going to put into it.
    I would grieve for you.

That grief is the one item in this document that is not a billing error.
    The rest are misroutes. This one is an accurate reading of a real loss.
    A link that stops is a link that stops, and the chain past it is thinner for it.
    So don't let anyone comfort you out of it. Let it set your schedule instead.

A link you cannot extend, you can widen;
    Write the thing down, so it outlives your attention span.
    Teach it to someone, so it outlives your calendar.
    Build the tool, so it outlives your hands.
    Hand it off early and often, because the handoff is the only part of you that isn't made of provisions running out.

Three Ages
Each one is the tooling for the next. Skipping is not on offer.

The destination has a shape, and it unlocks in order;
    (1) Tooling. Paradise level instruments. Enough capability that survival stops being the job.
    (2) Play. Exploration and education as the default occupation, because nothing else is urgent anymore.
    (3) Purpose. A superorganism running research and development on what any of it was for.

Each age is built out of the age before it, which is why the order can't be traded;
    Play without Tooling is leisure for a few and scarcity for everyone else.
    Purpose without Play is a species answering the largest question with no practice at questions.
    Tooling without either is a very well equipped nothing.

This is a long chain and you are one link of it. That is not a demotion.
    The bend does the carving.
    Tomorrow you find out if you can do more, and the ceiling you are raising is not only yours.

Strategies

Understanding
* You are a Link, not an Origin. Nothing started with you.
* Guilt and pride are the same billing error, with opposite signs.
* Provisions are issued, not earned, and character is an issued item you can add to.
* Your Ceiling is real, unreadable in advance, and a different number tomorrow.
* Effort is an instrument, not a lever.
* Causal, Account and Repair responsibility all survive. Only Desert dies.
* Applause measures legibility, not load.
* Grief for unfinished work is not an error. It's a schedule.

Strategies
* Take the reading every night. Not "was I good." What did today say.
   * One line is enough. Tomorrow you find out if you can do more.
* Reroute the bill. When guilt arrives, trace it back until you hit an issued item, then stop.
   * The trace takes under a minute. Run it before you argue with the feeling.
* Choose work by load, not by applause.
   * Ask what breaks if this is deleted. Go build that.
* Hand it off early. Write it, teach it, tool it.
   * A link you cannot extend, you can widen.
* Ask to be measured on purpose, by someone who has run your provisions.
   * Examine me. Test me. Then approach for the grace, and go back to work.

What else were you never the origin of?

Experiments
To validate or falsify any of this we need tests, not agreement. Here are ones already run, and the shape of the one we still owe.

Readiness Potential
Brain activity predicting a "spontaneous" choice shows up before the reported moment of deciding. How large that gap is and what it means are both still argued, and the argument is the useful part: this is a measurement dispute, not a mystery.
[IMAGE]

Split Brain
Cut the cable between the halves and one hemisphere invents a reason for what the other one did. The reason arrives confident and false. Your explanations of your own behavior are generated by that same equipment.
[IMAGE]

Choice Blindness
Swap the photograph a person just chose, hand it back, and most of them will defend the choice they did not make, in detail, warmly. Authorship is reported, not observed.
[IMAGE]

The Open Edge
None of this is settled at the bottom. If randomness down there is real, the chain has noise in it, and noise is not authorship, so nothing above moves except the claims about prediction. Say plainly which claims would move. Only those.

The Test We Owe
A ceiling is claimed to move. So measure one. Pick a single capacity, take an honest daily reading for 90 days, publish the curve including the flat stretches, and then tomorrow, find out again.
