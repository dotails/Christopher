# The Vulcan carved under the stone

In Vulcan, the carving under the stone is Golic Vulcan - the language of Surak's Gol, as the
Vulcan Language Institute set it down - and not English spelled in Vulcan letters. Every word is
from the Institute's dictionaries (its English-to-Golic and Golic-to-English dictionaries, kept
online at [vli.surak.nu](https://vli.surak.nu/vli-default.php)), put together by its grammar
(its [grammar](https://vli.surak.nu/grammar.php) and [lessons](https://vli.surak.nu/lessons.php)).
A few things the dictionaries have no word for are built from their words the way the
dictionaries build their own: those are marked *built* below. `tools/vulcan.test.mjs` checks that
every word the carving can cut is listed here.

## How it is put together

- **The verb comes first**, then who does it, then what to, then the rest: *ip-sut nezhak ek'kal*,
  hides (the) chaos (a) sphere - *the jumble hides a sphere*. There is no *the*, and *a* is left
  out, as Modern Golic does.
- **Prepositions are prefixes**, set off by the half-stop: *svi'nezhak*, in the chaos; *fi'kel-feklar*,
  on the struts; *t'ish-veh*, of it, its. What is owned comes before its owner.
- **More than one** is *~lar* (*behsu*, neighbour; *behsular*, neighbours), but never after a
  number: *keh yeht-behsu*, four true neighbours.
- **An adjective joined to its noun** comes before it, with a stroke (*uzh-shid*, a new form);
  standing after its noun it says what the noun is (*suk-pakuv ikapik*, the hoop is closed).
- **To bid**, *'uh* follows the verb's root: *tal-tor*, find, *tal'uh*, find!; *hutau*, shake, *huta'uh*.
- **The past**: *~au* becoming *~al* (*terau*, join, *teral*, joined; *hoknau*, gather, *hoknal*);
  with *ki'*, has been done (*ki'teral*, already joined).
- **Numbers** count as the Institute's tables do: *wuh, dah, reh, keh, kau, sheh, steh, oh, nau,
  leh* (one to ten), *leh-wuh* (eleven), *dah-leh* (twenty), *dah-leh kau* (twenty-five), *teh* (a
  hundred), *leh-teh* (a thousand), *ris* (nothing). First, second, ...: *wuhr-, dahr-, rehr-*. So
  *steh t'nau*, seven of nine, is a seat's score of 7/9.
- **Solids are named by their faces**, as the dictionary names *keh-muk*, the tetrahedron (four
  faces), and *sheh-muk*, the cube (six); cut at the corners, *snem-* (removed), as it names
  *snem-sheh-muk*, the truncated cube. The kal-toh's two concentric icosidodecahedra, of thirty-two
  faces each, are *dah ka-svitanik reh-leh dah-muk*. Flat shapes are named by their sides:
  *reh-vla*, the triangle; *keh-vla*, the square.

## Its marks

The Vulcan fonts carry letters only, so Golic's three marks are cut by hand among them: the
**stroke** (*pakh*, -), joining a word's roots or, standing alone, parting a sentence as a comma
would; the **double stroke** (*dah-pakh*, written -- here), standing for a colon; and the
**half-stop** (*ulef-pekhaya*, '), setting a prefix or ending apart from its word.
