# Style forensics — *Consciousness*

Notes behind `dotails-document-engine.md` v2. Everything here was read off the *Consciousness* document itself, then checked against the v1 engine. Two v1 rules contradicted the source and were corrected.

## Corrections to v1

**1. The hedge rule was backwards.**
v1: "Delete every hedge on sight: perhaps, maybe, arguably... zero instances."
Source: the document hedges constantly, and deliberately — "It is possible that neuron networks can process the functional processing demands", "It is possible there are deeper level quantum vibrations", "This would be less likely or more difficult to develop gradually". Meanwhile its own claims carry zero hedge: "So if you feel like you are presently aware you aren't. You physically can't."
Corrected to a **placement** law: no hedges on your claims, mandatory hedges on weighed candidates. The contrast is what makes the certainty credible.

**2. The analogy rule was backwards.**
v1: "An analogy may appear only when it is provably the shortest accurate explanation, and then only for one sentence. Never sustained. Never architecture."
Source: the computational frame runs the entire document — tags, search, compute, delete cycle, weak clocks, batteries, plug and play, backups, boot up clones, "look at your own code". It is architecture.
Corrected by distinguishing a **substrate claim** (asserted as literally true, defensible in an argument, allowed to run the whole document) from a decorative simile (banned). "feelings are thoughts, processing, ideas, conclusions, data" is not a comparison.

## Additions

**The One Engine.** The document's real spine is not its axis map, it is one mechanism applied to nine domains: the brain trades accuracy for cheapness. Emotions are compressed summaries because raw sensor data is expensive. Terds outsource thinking because thinking is expensive. Goalpost-shifting protects the mystery because re-founding beliefs is expensive. Thinking errors are shortcuts. Every strategy at the end is the same counter-move — spend the energy, you can afford it now. v1 had no step for finding this, which is why v1 output reads like chapters instead of one idea.

**The Refrain.** "We can now afford to think" and its variants appear six times across the document, never announced. It is how a mesh survives serialization.

**Gloss direction.** v1 had `word(gloss)`; it missed which way round. The sacred word goes outside, the engineering word inside: `Soul(data)`, `Save(recover)`, `will(want)`, `remembering(searching)`, `computed(thought through)`. That single move is the thesis running at sentence level — the mystery keeps its name and loses its magic.

**The Arithmetic Ladder.** Component figures stacked, multiplied on the page, landing on a total that is *impossible* (7 billion messages a second), which then proves the system must be doing something other than what the reader assumed. v1 asked for numbers but not for derivation.

**The Trojan Parallel.** Déjà vu proven first (mislabel a recent memory as #LongAgo), then the identical sentence re-run on the sacred case (mislabel it as #NOW). The near-exact copy is the argument.

**The Dial.** Three binaries converted to continuous variables — conscious, alive, free. "You can be more or less Conscious/Free." This is the most characteristic single operation in the document.

**Also added:** The Dissolve ("this doesn't even matter, because you defined it wrong" — free will as freedom from *other wills*, not from physics), The Existence Proof ("If it has been made it can be made"), The Pre-Closed Exit (naming the goalpost dodge before making the claim), The Public Coining (performing the word search on the page: "Tribes of Humans? Herds of Sheep? uh Terds"), The Assumption Audit (each list item carries its own "assuming...", then the tally), The Fun-Fact Transfer (computers have batteries for the same reason), The Precision Downgrade ("But the stories are not true. Not true enough at least."), and the concession clause requirement — every weighed candidate carries a "Though...".

**Skeleton.** The macro-arc was made explicit: contents → opening pledge → definitions and functions → implications and applications → strategies → experiments → open questions. Two things v1 lacked: the **Strategies** page is the document compressing itself into its own index (one line per section), and the document ends on **falsification plus unanswered questions**, not on a call to action.

**Testimony demoted.** v1 defaulted it on. *Consciousness* contains none — first person appears twice, for a heard claim and a stated hope. Default is now off.

**No-Consensus-Lie branch.** v1 assumed every topic hides an inversion. On a procedural or genuinely settled topic a forced inversion reads as parody, so v2 switches the document's job to the missing map instead.

## Surface details worth keeping

- Capitalization is the tagging mechanism in plain text: Causality Speed Limit, Weak Clocks, Summarized Story, Virtual Space, Essential Life.
- Section separator is a long run of underscores; every top-level section starts a new page.
- Image density is high — roughly one to three slots per section, often carrying the argument.
- The signature list: stem ending in a semicolon, then items each opening with a capitalized present-tense verb ("Optimizes... Compresses and Consolidates... Cleans... Preprocesses... Prioritizes and Tags...").
- Contents page is bare: titles only, no numbers, no commentary.
- The best Plug in the document is a joke and a test at once: `24 + 48+96+192=`, `96 x 192=`, `192 ÷ 97=`, `The Ultimate Purpose of the Universe=`.
